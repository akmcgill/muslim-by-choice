  • Searching online? Check firstChecklist

    A lot of online content about Islam is unreliable or harsh. Before you trust a source:

    • Start with the resources in this class.
    • Check who is teaching and where they studied.
    • Be cautious of content that mocks other Muslims or says only one school of thought is valid.
    • Bring what you find to class and ask.
