Answered questions

  • Do I need to sign up for class?

    No. Drop in any week, in person or online. You don't need to know anything first, and you're welcome to just listen.

  • I'm not Muslim. Can I still come?

    Yes. Questions from people exploring Islam are welcome every week, with no commitment.

  • What should I wear?

    Loose, modest clothing. Women may want a scarf for the prayer hall. Shoes come off in the prayer hall.

    Committee: review wording

  • What should I learn first?

    Start with Surat al-Fatiha, then wudu and one prayer step by step. Everything is in Classwork, in order.

  • Will other people see my phone number?

    No. Class updates go out through an announcements channel where followers can't see each other's numbers.
